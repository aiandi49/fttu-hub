HOW THIS STARTED

Two sisters, one long phone call, and a question worth building around.

F.T.T.U began as a conversation about where money actually goes once it leaves your hand. Not a boycott. Not a slogan. A simple redirect: when there's a choice — and there almost always is — send the business to an institution owned by the people it serves.

This hub opened with banking, because a deposit decides its own future — whether it becomes a mortgage, a small-business loan, a scholarship, or just another line on someone else's balance sheet. Now it includes farmers too, so you can order meat, eggs, and produce straight from the family that raised it, no grocery store in between. Every other industry gets added the same way: verified first, one at a time.

"From them… to us…" isn't about where you can't go. It's about where you can go first.

Five banks to start with

Five cities, five different histories, one thing in common: they're owned by the community banking with them. Move a checking account, open a savings account, or just ask them about a small-business loan.

WASHINGTON, D.C. & LOS ANGELES, CA

City First Bank

A community development bank built to move capital into low- and moderate-income neighborhoods — affordable housing, nonprofits, and small businesses that traditional lenders pass over.

  • Founded: 1998 (as a CDFI in D.C.)
  • Status: Largest Black-led minority depository institution in the U.S.
  • Also: Certified B Corp
Visit City First Bank

DURHAM, NORTH CAROLINA

M&F Bank

Mechanics and Farmers Bank has stood in Durham's Black Wall Street since 1907 — one of the oldest Black-owned banks in the country, and still lending there today.

  • Founded: 1907
  • Status: One of the two oldest Black-owned banks in the U.S.
  • 2026 update: Merging with Optus Bank (SC) to form the nation's largest Black-owned financial institution
Visit M&F Bank

CHICAGO, ILLINOIS

GN Bank

Chicago's last remaining Black-owned bank, tracing back to a savings and loan founded in the 1930s so Black families could get mortgages when nobody else would write them one.

  • Founded: 1934 (as Illinois Service Federal)
  • Status: One of roughly a dozen Black-owned banks left nationally
  • Focus: Personal banking, mortgages, small-business lending
Visit GN Bank

NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA

Liberty Bank & Trust

Chartered in 1972 by Dr. Norman C. Francis and Alden McDonald Jr., Liberty grew through acquisitions into the largest Black-owned bank in the country, now with branches across 11 states.

  • Founded: 1972
  • Status: Largest Black-owned bank in the U.S. by assets
  • Footprint: 11 states, including LA, MI, IL, AL
Visit Liberty Bank

ATLANTA, GEORGIA

Citizens Trust Bank

Opened in 1921 on Auburn Avenue by five Black businessmen known as "the Fervent Five," after one of them was turned away from a store for a pair of socks.

  • Founded: 1921
  • Status: First Black-owned bank in the FDIC (1934) and Federal Reserve (1947)
  • Today: One of the largest Black-owned banks in the U.S.
Visit Citizens Trust Bank

Black-owned farms & ranches, direct to your door

No grocery store in between. Every farm below sells straight to individual households — meat, eggs, and produce shipped or delivered from the family that actually raised it.

HAMPTON, GEORGIA

Swanson Family Farm

Farmer Wayne & the Swanson family, since 2008

A 32-acre pasture farm raising grass-fed beef, lamb, goat, and pork the way Wayne's grandparents farmed in New Jersey — no commercial feed, no steroids, no hormones.

  • Beef, lamb, goat, pork
  • Ships nationwide
  • Farm pickup available

How to order: Shop bulk beef (1/8, 1/4, whole cow) and cuts directly on their site; local pickup in Hampton, GA.

Visit Swanson Family Farm

PLANT CITY, FLORIDA

Ferguson Farms

Family-owned, pasture to doorstep

Grass-fed beef, pasture-raised poultry, and wild-caught seafood, raised without GMOs, antibiotics, or added hormones, shipped frozen for freshness.

  • Beef, poultry, seafood
  • Ships nationwide
  • Subscription boxes

How to order: Shop online for farm boxes or à la carte cuts; free shipping over $159, with recurring "Subscribe & Save" delivery.

Visit Ferguson Farms

OAKWOOD, TEXAS

Ratcliff Premium Meats

Caney Creek Ranch — the Ratcliff family

A woman-owned, Black-owned ranch in East Texas run by Kimberly Ratcliff, a USDA Minority Farm Advisory Committee appointee. Pasture-raised, 21-day dry-aged, USDA-inspected beef, ranch to freezer.

  • Grass-fed beef
  • Ships nationwide, frozen
  • Custom cut sheets

How to order: Build a custom box, subscribe monthly, or order a half/whole cow directly on their site.

Visit Ratcliff Premium Meats

NORTHERN NECK, VIRGINIA

Blackbird Farms

A cooperative of first-generation Black & Indigenous farmers

Built by farmer Chris Newman and partner farms (including Sylvanaqua Farms) around a shared belief: production and distribution should be owned by the community, not agribusiness. Pasture-raised chicken, eggs, pork, and beef.

  • Chicken, eggs, pork, beef
  • Ships to the continental U.S.
  • Local door-to-door in VA/DC/MD

How to order: Ground beef and scrapple ship anywhere in the continental U.S.; the fuller product line delivers direct within their DMV-area route.

Visit Blackbird Farms

OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA

Farms to Grow, Inc.

Connecting you to a whole network of California growers

A nonprofit founded in 2004 by Dr. Gail P. Myers that links home cooks directly to Black farmers across California through seasonal produce subscriptions — no grocery store markup in between.

  • Seasonal produce & value-add goods
  • CSA subscription boxes
  • Bay Area pickup & delivery

How to order: Subscribe to a CSA "Harvest Bag" online; email marketing@farmstogrow.com for wholesale or outside the Bay Area.

Visit Farms to Grow

ST. HELENA ISLAND, SOUTH CAROLINA

Gullah Farmers Cooperative

Ten Gullah Geechee farm families, one shared packhouse

A cooperative of small Gullah farms on South Carolina's Sea Islands, growing okra, sweet potatoes, greens, and other Lowcountry staples while preserving Gullah Geechee farming tradition.

  • Produce & Lowcountry staples
  • Currently bulk / wholesale
  • Serves the Carolinas & Georgia

How to order: Their packhouse is built for schools, hospitals, and retailers right now — reach out directly to ask about ordering as an individual or community group.

Visit Gullah Farmers Co-op
