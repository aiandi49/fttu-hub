WHAT'S REAL IN THIS PROTOTYPE

Live: this chat calls a real backend (/api/chat) which calls the real Claude API, reasoning against F.T.T.U's actual directory data (5 banks, 6 farms) embedded in this page. Every reply is generated fresh — nothing here is scripted.

Voice is fully local: the microphone uses your browser's built-in speech recognition (Web Speech API) to turn your voice into text right on your device — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere until you hit send, and it needs Chrome, Edge, or Safari (not currently supported in Firefox).

Not yet real: this can't open an account, place an order, or message a business for you — it hands you the right destination and the reason, then the link takes you the rest of the way.

To make the "Live" part actually live on your domain: deploy the /api/chat.js file alongside this page (same repo, Vercel turns it into a serverless function automatically), then add an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables. That key comes from console.anthropic.com and is billed separately, pay-per-use — it is not the same as a claude.ai subscription. Until that's set, this chat will tell you plainly what's missing instead of failing silently.
