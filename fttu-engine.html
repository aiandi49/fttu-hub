What's real on this page

Live answers. The chat sends your conversation to a small backend (/api/chat) that calls the Claude API. Claude reasons only over the directory embedded in this page — 5 banks and 6 farms — and every reply is written fresh. The three cards fill from that same directory data the moment a match is named, so nothing on the right is invented.

Voice stays on your device. The mic uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to turn speech into text before you send it. It works in Chrome, Edge, and Safari; Firefox doesn't support it yet, so the mic hides itself there.

What it can't do yet. It can't open an account, place an order, or contact a business for you. It names the right place, says why, and hands you the official link.

Turning it on. Deploy api/chat.js in the same Vercel project as this page, add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY under Project → Settings → Environment Variables, and redeploy. Keys come from console.anthropic.com and are billed per use, separately from any claude.ai plan. Until then, the chat says exactly what's missing.
